Before you start
  • All numbers in this app — standard drinks, driving-wait time, hangover outlook — are rough estimates based on general averages. They are not a measurement of your actual blood alcohol level and cannot account for your body, health, or how you feel.
  • This app does not provide medical, legal, or professional advice, and is not a substitute for a breathalyzer or your own judgment.
  • Never drive after drinking based on this app's estimate alone. When in doubt, don't drive — get a ride instead.
  • You are solely responsible for your own decisions, including whether and when it's safe for you to drive.
